How the code PSIB0021511 is built

PSIB is the bank part: the letters of Punjab & Sind Bank. The fifth character is always the digit 0. 021511 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 110023164 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 110 is the city, 023 is the bank and 164 is the branch.
